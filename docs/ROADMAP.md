# Development roadmap

Stages 0–5 are implemented and verified; Stage 6 groundwork and the local part of Stage 8 are complete; Stage 7 is gated. The user's Stage 4 semantic-retrieval specification replaces the previous milestone ordering. Retrieval evaluation labels remain provisional at the user's request; no independently human-reviewed benchmark quality is claimed. Later stages describe possible acceptance targets, not implemented functionality.
